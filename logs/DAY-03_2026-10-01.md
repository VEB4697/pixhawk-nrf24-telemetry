## Problems / findings
- Fake RF24 first treated any repeated 2-bit PID as a duplicate; the real chip needs PID **and** CRC (payload) to match. Fixed in both fakes; risk text corrected.
- Pattern checker misread a >127-byte purge gap as a "duplicate" (8-bit pattern wrap) -> added `PatternChecker::resync()`; outage tests now also assert "no stale burst" via rate bounds.
- Pin diagram: first render had overlapping labels/crossings; re-routed (nRF24 pin order chosen so SPI wires do not cross) and re-checked.
